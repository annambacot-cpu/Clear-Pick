# ClearPick ethical-design audit

Audited: 2026-07-22

## Product boundaries

- No real-money wagering, deposits, withdrawals, stakes, payouts, or sportsbook connections.
- Market references provide prediction context only and are labeled as having no stake or payout.
- No virtual currency or cash-like points.
- No casino imagery, sounds, celebratory wagering animations, or near-miss effects.
- No artificial deadlines, countdowns, odds boosts, limited-time offers, or fear-of-missing-out notifications.
- No loss-chasing language, punishing streaks, or rewards tied to prediction volume or risk.
- No infinite scroll. Game sections and history are finite and user-directed.

## Responsible feedback

- Decision Score weighting is transparent: up to 90 points for process and 10 for a correct outcome.
- Incorrect outcomes do not erase strong process scores.
- Completing replays does not award engagement points.
- Reflection prompts reward evidence, patience, awareness, and restraint.
- Behavioral conclusions require at least three settled predictions in each comparison group.
- Smaller qualifying samples are labeled `Possible pattern`; stronger samples are labeled `Pattern`.
- Missing injuries, records, statistics, and provider data are shown as unavailable rather than invented.

## User control and privacy

- Favorites and followed games are user-controlled.
- Live refresh exists only to update game information; it does not create urgency.
- Notifications are not requested.
- Hosted prediction history uses an anonymous device identifier and does not collect names, email addresses, payment information, or sportsbook credentials.
- Device-only fallback is disclosed whenever the hosted database is unavailable.
